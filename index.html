The Gap We Fill

What Colleges Are Dealing With — Right Now

TPOs and Principals face a structural problem: soft-skill vendors are everywhere, but no one is bringing real, current industry intelligence into the institution.

01

No Industry Intelligence Feed

Placement teams lack access to current hiring trends, AI-driven workforce shifts and employer skill expectations — making it hard to prepare students effectively.

→ AttiNexis delivers monthly intelligence briefings tailored to your placement context.
02

Expert Sessions Are a Logistics Nightmare

Finding, scheduling and coordinating industry professionals falls entirely on already-stretched faculty and TPOs — and it often doesn't happen at all.

→ We handle identification, onboarding, coordination and reporting — end to end.
03

Faculty Are Disconnected from Industry

Faculty guide students on career choices without regular exposure to how industries are actually evolving — creating a silent credibility gap in the classroom.

→ Our faculty briefings and enablement sessions bridge this directly.
04

Soft-Skill Vendors Don't Solve the Real Problem

Most colleges already have a soft-skills training partner. What's missing is someone who brings structured industry engagement, not generic communication workshops.

→ AttiNexis is positioned as an intelligence and engagement partner, not a vendor.
05

NAAC / NIRF Pressure Is Rising

Accreditation frameworks increasingly demand evidence of industry linkages, employer engagement and student employability outcomes — areas most colleges struggle to document.

→ Our structured programs generate the engagement evidence colleges need.
06

No Structured Corporate Hiring Pipeline

Students miss out on internships, live projects and PPO pathways because no one is systematically building corporate relationships on the college's behalf.

→ We connect colleges with structured corporate opportunities through our network.
Core Services

Three Things We Do — Exceptionally Well

Our three core services are what set AttiNexis apart. Everything else we offer builds on top of these.

Industry Intelligence

We research and synthesise what's happening in hiring, AI, and workforce evolution — and deliver it directly to your placement team and faculty in a format they can act on.

  • Monthly intelligence briefings
  • AI-led workforce change updates
  • High-growth sectors and roles
  • Employer skill expectations
  • Entry-level salary and hiring trends
Outcome: Your team makes better program, training and placement decisions — backed by current data, not assumptions.

Managed Expert Connect

We manage the complete industry expert engagement process — so your faculty and TPO team gets the benefit without the operational burden.

  • Expert identification and onboarding
  • Session planning and topic alignment
  • Expert coordination and follow-up
  • Student engagement facilitation
  • Post-session reporting and feedback
Outcome: Reliable, high-quality industry access — without increasing your team's workload. Our panel spans 5 to 42 years of experience.

Faculty & Placement Enablement

We support your faculty and placement team with current industry frameworks, so they can guide students with confidence and reduce their own operational burden.

  • Faculty industry briefing sessions
  • AI awareness and digital shifts
  • Recruiter expectation updates
  • Student readiness frameworks
  • Placement preparation support
Outcome: Faculty and placement teams guide students more effectively — grounded in what employers actually want today.

Our Founder

Where Academic Rigour Meets Corporate Reality

AttiNexis was founded on a direct observation — colleges are not lacking in effort, they are lacking in structured, current industry intelligence. With a PhD and over a decade of corporate exposure, Dhanashri built AttiNexis to be the external industry arm that institutions need but rarely have.

She engages directly with college leadership, TPOs and faculty — understanding each institution's unique context before co-designing an engagement model that delivers measurable outcomes.
